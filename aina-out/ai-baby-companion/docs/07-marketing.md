# Marketing — AI Baby Companion

> Stage 07 · Marketing · Brand → Copy → Visual → Pack. Builds on [03-ideation-prd](03-ideation-prd.md). Companion file: `app/marketing.html`.

## Positioning

**Category:** screen-free AI companion toy for young children, with parent-grade privacy.

**One-liner:** *A talking friend for your kid — and a control panel for you.*

**Target persona — "Cautious Parent Priya":** parent of a 3–7-year-old, uneasy about tablet time, even more uneasy about a mic in the bedroom. Wants imaginative, screen-free play *and* receipts on exactly what's captured.

**Positioning statement:** For parents who want less screen time without giving up control, AI Baby Companion is a voice-first plush toy whose every word and recording the parent can see, limit, and delete — unlike smart speakers, it's built to a children's-privacy bar from the first chip.

**Voice:** warm, plain, trustworthy. Reassuring, never hype-y. We sell *trust and calm*, not "AI magic."

## Differentiators (what to lead with)

1. **Consent-first, not consent-buried.** No mic or camera works until you say so — enforced on our servers, not just hidden in the app.
2. **You can read it and erase it.** Every conversation is yours to review, export, or delete in one tap.
3. **Screen-free by design.** No glowing rectangle — just a character that talks, listens, and remembers.
4. **Personality you choose.** Curious Explorer, Gentle Storyteller, and more — tuned to your child.

## Copy

**Hero headline:** *Screen-free play your kid will love — and you can trust.*
**Subhead:** A plush companion that talks, listens, and remembers — with a parent dashboard that puts consent, history, and the delete button in your hands.

**Feature blurbs (outcomes, not features):**
- *Know exactly what was said.* Every conversation, encrypted and yours to review or wipe.
- *Turn the mic off from your phone.* Per-child mic and camera switches, enforced instantly.
- *A friend that grows with them.* Pick a personality; the toy remembers your child between chats.
- *Delete everything, anytime.* Export or erase your child's data in one tap — no support ticket.

**Email (welcome):** *Subject — Your companion is ready (and so are you).* Body: walk the parent through pairing, granting consent, and where the delete button lives. Lead with control.

**Social (one post):** "Less screen time shouldn't mean less control. Our companion is screen-free *and* the only one where you can read every conversation and delete it in a tap. 👶🔒"

## Visual system

- **Palette:** soft, calm — warm cream background, a friendly teal/sky primary, a single coral accent for the "you're in control" CTAs. No neon, no harsh gradients (voice = calm, not hype).
- **Type:** Inter (matches the console); large, rounded headings; generous spacing.
- **Imagery:** the plush character + a parent's phone showing the controls, side by side — the two-audience story in one frame.
- **Components:** rounded cards, a trust-badge row (COPPA · GDPR-K · Encrypted · Screen-free), one primary CTA.

## Channels & funnel

- **Top:** parenting communities, screen-time-anxiety content, "is this safe?" SEO.
- **Mid:** the privacy story as the conversion lever — landing page leads with control, not AI.
- **Proof:** the export/erase demo is the trust closer. Show it.
