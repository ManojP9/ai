This product captures a young child's voice and (optionally) camera, so privacy is load-bearing. Here is exactly how it is governed:

Consent first

No mic or camera processing happens without verifiable parental consent. Requests return 403 until consent exists.

Per-sensor control

Microphone and camera switches per child, enforced server-side on every request.

Encryption at rest

Conversation content is encrypted (AES-256-GCM in production); HTTPS/TLS in transit.

Retention

Conversation turns auto-expire after {{ retention }} days via a daily job.

Access (export)

Download everything stored for a child as JSON, any time.

Erasure
