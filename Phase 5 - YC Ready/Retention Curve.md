# Retention Curve

**Why**: Retention is the only metric that proves product-market fit. If users return week 3, the product solved a real problem.

## Target

- Week 1 retention: ≥ 60%
- Week 2 retention: ≥ 40%
- Week 3 retention: ≥ 25%

A flat retention curve after week 3 is what YC looks for.

## How to Achieve It

- Personalized recommendations ([[Profile-Aware AI]]) make each visit feel tailored
- Weekly email digest ([[Weekly Digest]]) brings users back passively
- Ratings + journal ([[Ratings]]) make the app feel like their personal food history

## Tracking

- PostHog retention cohort report
- Export PNG for YC application
