# 10%+ Week-over-Week Growth

**Why**: YC's benchmark for "good growth" in consumer is 5–7% WoW. 10% puts you in the top tier of applicants.

## How to Sustain It

- Each referral signup contributes to WoW growth
- New city leaderboard pages bring steady organic traffic
- Weekly digest re-activates users who would otherwise churn

## Tracking

- PostHog weekly active users cohort
- WAU growth rate = (WAU this week - WAU last week) / WAU last week × 100
