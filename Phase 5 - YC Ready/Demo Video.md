# 1-Minute Demo Video

**Why**: YC partners watch hundreds of demo videos. A clear, fast, no-narration demo that shows the product working is more persuasive than any pitch.

## Script (60 seconds)

1. **0:00–0:05** — Open app on phone. Show home screen.
2. **0:05–0:15** — Type "spicy thai food". Show Claude thinking animation. Results appear.
3. **0:15–0:25** — Tap ❤️ on Pad Thai. Navigate home. Show saved favorites.
4. **0:25–0:35** — Go to /progress. Show charts (donut + bar). Tap a task to update status.
5. **0:35–0:50** — Show PostHog dashboard: MAU, growth %, search events.
6. **0:50–0:60** — Show Stripe dashboard: MRR. End on app logo.

## Tools

- Record on iPhone (4K, landscape)
- Edit in CapCut or iMovie (no voiceover needed — let the product speak)
- Background music: subtle lo-fi
